What is a carbon footprint?

A carbon footprint is the total greenhouse gas emissions caused by a person's activities, expressed in kilograms or tonnes of CO₂-equivalent (CO₂e) per year. It covers travel, energy, food, the things you buy and the electronics you throw away.

ECOFOOTPRINT is an educational project. Results are estimates, not official measurements.
